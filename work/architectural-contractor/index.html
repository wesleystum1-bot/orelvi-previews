Questions

Before you call

Can you repair it instead of replacing it?

Often, yes. If the deck is sound and the failure is localised, a repair is the honest answer and we will say so.

How long does a typical re-roof take?

A standard single-family tear-off and re-roof is usually two to three working days, weather permitting.

Do you work with insurance claims?

We document damage with photographs and a written scope. We do not negotiate the claim for you, but the packet is built for an adjuster.

What happens to the old roof?

Hauled the same day. We magnet-sweep the drive and beds before we leave the site.
