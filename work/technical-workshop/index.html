02

Method

04 stages
  1. Stage 01 / 04

    Choose by the numbers

    Samples go on your own glass next to each other with the VLT, TSER and IR rejection for each, so you are buying heat rejection and not a shade of grey.

  2. Stage 02 / 04

    Cut the pattern

    Plotted from the vehicle pattern on the bench. Nothing is trimmed on the car, so the glass, the seals and the defroster grid are never cut against.

  3. Stage 03 / 04

    Shrink and fit

    Rear glass is heat-shrunk on the outside first, then set from inside in one piece. Door panels come off where the film has to tuck under the belt seal.

  4. Stage 04 / 04

    Cure

    Windows stay up three to five days while the mounting solution dries. Haze and small water pockets in that window are normal and we tell you when they should be gone.

Questions

04 answered
Q 01

How dark can I legally go in Texas?

Front side windows have to let at least 25% of the light through, and the windshield is limited to a strip down to the AS-1 line. Behind the driver on an SUV or truck you can go as dark as you want. We fit the fronts legal and note the VLT on the invoice.

Q 02

Is ceramic worth the difference over carbon?

If the car parks outside in this heat, yes — ceramic rejects far more infrared at the same visible darkness, so the cabin cools quicker. If it lives under cover and you mainly want privacy, carbon does the job.

Q 03

Why is my new tint hazy and bubbled?

That is mounting solution still drying between the film and the glass. In this humidity it clears in three to five days, longer in a cool spell. Leave the windows up until it does.

Q 04

Can you film over factory privacy glass?

Yes, and it stacks. Factory privacy glass is tinted in the pane, so 20% film over it reads much darker than 20%. We hold the sample against your own rear glass first so you can see the combination.
