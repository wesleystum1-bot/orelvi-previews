Questions

Before you sit down

  1. 01

    Do you take walk-ins?

    When a chair is free. Appointments come first on Fridays and Saturdays.

  2. 02

    How long does a cut take?

    Forty minutes. An hour if there is a beard with it.

  3. 03

    Do you cut children’s hair?

    Yes, from about six, when sitting still is realistic.

In the chair

How an appointment runs

  1. 01

    Sit down

    We talk about what grew out badly last time before anything is picked up.

  2. 02

    Cut

    Scissor over comb where the shape needs it, clippers where it does not.

  3. 03

    Finish

    Washed, dried and dressed so you leave with it sitting the way it will sit tomorrow.
