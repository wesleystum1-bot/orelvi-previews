Questions

Good to know

Do I need a consultation first?

For any colour change, yes. It is free and it stops a two-visit job being sold as one.

How long does balayage take?

Three to four hours including the toner. Bring something to do.

Can you fix a home colour?

Usually. Book the correction consultation rather than a standard colour so we have the time.

The appointment

How a visit runs

  1. I

    Consultation

    Fifteen minutes with dry hair, a photograph of where you want to get to, and an honest answer about how many visits that is.

  2. II

    Colour

    Applied and watched. Timers are a starting point, not the decision.

  3. III

    Cut and finish

    Cut after colour so the shape suits where the light actually falls.

  4. IV

    Home routine

    What to wash it with and how often, written down, because that is most of whether it lasts.
