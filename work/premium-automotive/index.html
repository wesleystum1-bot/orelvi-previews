05

Before you book

Do I need correction before a coating?
A coating locks in whatever is underneath. If there are swirls, they are there for the life of the coating.
How long will you have the car?
Correction and coating is typically three to five days including cure. We book one car per bay.
Can I wash it myself afterwards?
Yes — two-bucket, pH-neutral, no brushes. We will show you the method at hand-back.
