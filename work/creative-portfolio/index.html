Method

How a project runs

  1. 01

    Site visit

    Before the shoot, to see which rooms work at which hour. It changes the schedule more than anything else.

  2. 02

    Shoot

    A day or two on site, tethered, with someone from the practice there to say what matters.

  3. 03

    Selects

    A contact sheet within three days. You choose, then those are finished.

  4. 04

    Delivery

    Full resolution and web-sized, with a licence that says plainly where you can use them.

One photographer, one assistant on larger jobs. Ten years photographing buildings, mostly for the people who drew them.
