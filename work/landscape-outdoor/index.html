How a garden gets made

4 stages. The order matters more than the speed.

  1. 01

    Walk the ground

    Slope, sun, drainage and what is already worth keeping. Usually an hour, on site.

  2. 02

    Draw it

    A measured plan and planting schedule. Yours to build with anyone.

  3. 03

    Build

    Hard landscape first, then soil, then planting — in that order, always.

  4. 04

    Establish

    Scheduled visits through the first two growing seasons, then a written handover.

Questions

Do you take the design without the build?

Yes. The drawings are yours and you can take them to any contractor.

When is the right time to start?

Hard landscape runs year-round here. Planting goes in autumn or early spring so roots take before summer.

Will it look finished on day one?

No, and it should not. Plants go in at a size that establishes. Year three is what the drawing shows.
