Questions

Before you call

  1. 01

    The old unit was four tons. Should the new one match?

    Not automatically. Most replacements around here are oversized, which short-cycles the compressor and leaves the humidity up in July. The load calculation settles it either way.

  2. 02

    Will a heat pump hold up in a Lancaster winter?

    In most houses, with the balance point set correctly and the strip heat locked out above it. We show the capacity at design temperature so you can see exactly where the backup starts to carry the load.

  3. 03

    Why do you keep talking about static pressure?

    It is the resistance the blower is fighting. Much above half an inch of water column the airflow falls off, the coil ices and the compressor pays for it. It takes ten minutes to measure.

  4. 04

    Do you offer a maintenance agreement?

    Two visits a year, one heating and one cooling, with the readings recorded each time. You do not need to hold one to get service from us.

  5. 05

    Can you work on the system somebody else installed?

    Yes. We start by measuring what it is doing now, which on a system that was never commissioned is the only way to know what is actually wrong with it.

How it works

From the call to the invoice

Request service (717) 555-0152

  1. Step 01

    Measure the house

    Room-by-room load calculation, duct static and the pressure drop across the filter, all before any equipment is discussed.

  2. Step 02

    Size and quote

    Model numbers, capacity at the local design temperature, and what has to change in the ductwork to actually deliver it.

  3. Step 03

    Install and commission

    Line set brazed with nitrogen flowing, system evacuated to 500 microns and held, charge weighed in and then verified running.

  4. Step 04

    Hand over the numbers

    The commissioning sheet — static, temperature split, subcooling and superheat — is yours at the end, so the next person to open the unit knows where it started.
