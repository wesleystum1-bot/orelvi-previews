How it works

From booking to a site you can open.

You spend five minutes on the form. We do the rest before you’re asked for anything.

  1. 1

    You book

    Your business name, your town, what you do. A current site if you have one.

  2. 2

    We research

    We read what’s already public about you: your services, hours, area and the photos you’ve posted.

  3. 3

    A finished site at a private link, checked on six screen sizes from a small phone to a wide desktop.

  4. 4

    Launch or walk away

    Ask for changes, launch it on your domain, or say no thanks. All three are fine.

House rules

What never goes on your site.

A site is only as good as what it can back up. If we can’t verify it, it stays off the page until you give it to us.

  • Made-up reviews or testimonials. Only real ones you point us to.
  • Invented awards, badges or years in business. Your record, or nothing.
  • Stock photos passed off as your work. Your own photos, or a designed placeholder that says what it is.
  • Fake counters and urgency. No invented customer counts, no countdown timers.
  • A contact form that goes nowhere. It reaches your inbox, or it’s a call button instead.

Questions

Before you book.

Is the proof really free?

Yes. You pay only if you decide to launch it. There’s no deposit and we don’t take card details at booking.

What if I already have a website?

Tell us where it is. We’ll use what’s accurate on it and design the new one from scratch, so you can compare the two side by side.

Do I need photos?

They help. We use photos you’ve already posted of your own work. Where there aren’t any, the page carries a designed placeholder until you send some.

Who owns the site?

Once you’ve paid to launch, it’s yours: your domain, your content, your contact form going to your inbox.

Can I ask for changes?

Yes. Tell us what to change and we’ll send you a revised proof before anything goes live.

Do you work with my trade?

Probably. The families above cover 29 trades, from roofing and detailing to dental and law. If yours isn’t listed, book anyway and tell us what you do.
